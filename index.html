The Craft

Small batches. Big care.

We roast on a single drum, in batches small enough to watch every second of the curve. Every lot is cupped before it's bagged, and every bag is packed by hand in kraft paper, with the roast date on the front.

  • Sourced directlyTraceable, fairly-priced lots from growers we know.
  • Roasted weeklyFresh to order. Never sitting on a shelf for months.
  • Brewed to tasteEvery coffee has a suggested brew guide on the bag.
